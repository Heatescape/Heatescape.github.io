Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Kitten & Lala Uploader is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The app requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and privacy status. The authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of any other person. No data is sent to any third party other than Google's YouTube API.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
